# Chapter 16 Paragon: End of Act 2

Chapter 16 is broken into three "parts". The first is titled **Between Heaven and Hell**.

**Relationship Point Choices**:
- Not recently... (-1 Venam)
- I haven't, no. (-1 Venam)
- ... (-3 Venam)

Accept the fast travel to the Grand Dream Stadium and head up. Before proceeding, the inner door will emphasize that things will change, once you pass through the threshold. You don't want to have any unfinished business. I too will emphasize this - *literally anything listed anywhere above in the walkthrough may or may not remain accessible after this point*. Areas, sidequests, items, Pokemon. So take extra care you've done everything you set out to do before proceeding into the Chapter 16 contents proper.
