# Chapter 16 Paragon: End of Act 2

Chapter 16 is broken into three "parts". The first is titled **Between Heaven and Hell**.

**Relationship Point Choices**:
- Not recently... (-1 Venam)
- I haven't, no. (-1 Venam)
- ... (-3 Venam)

Accept the fast travel to the Grand Dream Stadium and head up. Before proceeding, the inner door will emphasize that things will change, once you pass through the threshold. You don't want to have any unfinished business. I too will emphasize this - *literally anything listed anywhere above in the walkthrough may or may not remain accessible after this point*. Areas, sidequests, items, Pokemon. So take extra care you've done everything you set out to do before proceeding into the Chapter 16 contents proper.

Enter the Grand Dream Arena when you are fully ready!

**Relationship Point Choices**:
- Let's go. (+1 Venam)
- It'll be fine. (+2 Ren)
- Sucks, but we have to. (+3 Ren, +3 Venam)

## Past Aevium 4

After returning to the past:

**Relationship Point Choices**:
- Ren's right. (+3 Ren)
- We gotta stick together. (+1 Ren, +1 Venam)
- Control yourself, please. (-1 Ren, -2 Venam)

The path is dark, but straightforward. Go up.

!partner(["Ren", :OUTCAST, 9])

!dbattle(["Abigail", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], ["Anna", :ARCHSERVANT, 0])

Make your way up the stairs on the left to Nymiera. She will join us as a partner.

!partner(["Nymiera", :PROTECTOR_NYMIERA, 1])

!dbattle(["Eliza", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], ["Cornelia", :ARCHSERVANT, 0])

Accept the Archservant's quest. The first target is below.

!battle(["Mackenzie", :SERVANT, 0])

The second is in the room in which Venam resides presently.

!battle(["Bertha", :SERVANT, 0])

Enter the room to the left and use the Darchlight Crystal. Enter the upper right room next for the next Darchlight Crystal. The room on the lower right has the third, where you'll learn the code 142513. A simple cypher - 14-25-13 is NYM! Anyways, enter it in the fireplace and head down. Our third Servant can be found here.

!battle(["Adenine", :SERVANT, 0])

In the chest is *10 Exp. Candies XL*, a *Max Revive*, and *Focus Sash*. Interact with the Darchlight Crystal on the right side. Back upstairs, use the Darchlight Crystal again.

Return to the Archservant in the entry room. The storage room to the right will now be unlocked. Pick up an *Adamant Mint*, *Jolly Mint*, *EV Tuner*, *EV Booster*, and *Amulet Coin*. The sign on the wall highlights "Cherry Macaron" - back in the room down and right, we can talk to the eastern wall to input this password. Pick up the Clear Amulet (apparently a canon item... today I learned) and read the notebook. Enter the middle door upstairs, then use password "Chant du Sygne" or "Swan Song" downstairs to proceed.

As Maria, take a bath then talk to the chest, then make your way down the hallway as in the prologue. As yourself, choose whether or not to free Gardevoir's spirit.

**Relationship Point Choices**:
- Just act natural. (+2 Ren, +1 Venam)
- No need to worry. (+1 Ren, +2 Venam)
- We're screwed. (+1 Venam, -2 Ren)

Head down the left hallway.

!battle(["Olivia", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Blessed Field")

Defeat her to open the chest to get the *Golden Apple*, which essentially as the Pangoro or Zorua Crest when held! Head to the right.

!dbattle(["Chloé", :SERVANT, 0], ["Candace", :SERVANT, 0], "Blessed Field")

Heal, and proceed. You'll then enter a real-time battle segment as Nymiera, against Vitus. Spam attacks with the keys on the bottom to win these rounds. This will be interspersed with trainer fights for the Interceptor. A gauntlet of two fights with the "Non-fainted Pokemon will heal, Fainted Pokemon stay such" ruleset is in play:

!dbattle(["Elizabeth", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], ["Adina", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Blessed Field")

The second fight:

!partner(["Hazuki", :PROTECTOR_HAZUKI, 1])

!battle(["Bathsheba", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Blessed Field")

One more Nymiera/Vitus section follows, then we return to the gauntlet fully healed. There is a full heal between these two battles, as well, though Anju only joins us on the first.

!partner(["Anju", :PROTECTOR_ANJU, 1])

!battle(["Claudia", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Blessed Field")

!battle(["Esther", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Blessed Field")

This brings us to the third and fourth phases for Nym and Vitus. Spam to win, then spam the "Up" key when prompted.

### Fleeing Storm-Nym

Flee from Storm Nym through the path.

!battle(["Astra", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Dragon's Den")

Shortly after this fight, you'll be chased more directly. Save often! Getting caught means having to fight.

!battle(["Storm-Nym", :STORMNYM, 0], "Dragon's Den")

Carefully head down and go left. We'll get Vivian's power to remove obstacles. You can get *2 Ability Capsules* and a *EV Tuner* along the path east. A *Max Revive* is tricky to reach. 

!battle(["Magda", :ARCHSERVANT, 0], "Dragon's Den")

When the timer starts, head down to reach a *Focus Sash* and quickly run to the east! When you make it to the next room, walk up to talk to Anastasia, fix the rift below, then follow.

!boss(:ARCHRIFTGALVANTULA, "Dragon's Den")

Ren and Venam are more than capable partners for this fight. Proceed to the north when you are able. In "Viridian Forest" you can carefully pick up *3 Full Restores*, a *Focus Sash*, *10 Exp. Candies XL*. Continue following the path down the ladder.

!boss(:ARCHRIFTVOLCANION, "Glitch Field")

Another challenge that your allies can help you through.

Once you reach the lift, head back down and right to the dead end. The lift will work from here.

## Grand Dream City X

Good thing we escaped that near-death scenario in the past to return to a peaceful present.

!boss(:SENTAKA)

You'll observe you can put out fires by walking into them. We want to put out every single fire that we can reach, starting by going down and left in the area.

[New Game+ Content](#chapter-16-paragon-ng-part-2)

On the right side, emerge out of the Sewer System into Grand Dream City... X.

After chatting with the group here, make your way to the west. As long as your party contains a Water move, continue to put out fires as you proceed through the city. Heal at the Center and proceed.

!partner(["Ren", :OUTCAST, 9])

!battle(["Squad 01", :XENHORDE, 1], "City Field")

Head north into Central Square X. Through the west building, you can talk to the NPCs to **save lives** here. Suffice it to say, there is inevitably going to be a lot of destruction, but we can minimize that destruction. I will guide us through such that we do!

Head up through the building to the left.

!dbattle(["Eli and Sharon", :FORTUNATEWINGS, 0], "City Field")

Win to get the *Xen Emblem*. **Eli and Sharon will join the team**. Accept the fast travel and talk to the group that is here. **Kanon will join the team**, and we will unlock the "Analysis" ability. 

I'd recommend reading the dialogue on the objectives and rules in this section. I stressed earlier that completing *all* side content before starting Chapter 16 would be important - we will not be able to recruit everyone in this section without certain sidequest, completion, so keep that in mind. The guide from here will assume that you have!
